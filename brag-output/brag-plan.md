# Auralis brag plan

**What it is:** a cozy pixel-art study game you play with friends: a focus timer that pays a coin a minute, spent on furniture and pets for a shared pixel house.
**For:** students who study better with company. **Sets it apart:** your study timer *is* a little house your friends live in.
**Funniest true claim:** the baby dragon costs 2,000 coins, and coins are focused minutes, so it's ~33 hours of studying.
**Share caption:** "Every focused minute is a coin. The dragon is 2,000."

**Angle:** pay for a pixel dragon with pure focus. **Tone:** default (punchy, playful, clean), matching the game's calm, cozy feel.
**Visual identity:** the game's own palette (`#efe6da` paper with dot grid, `#2b2530` 3px ink outlines with solid offset shadows, `#fffaf3` cards, `#5b7fd6` blue, `#f2c14e` gold), Fredoka, the blocky timer font, and every sprite drawn live by `src/draw.ts` / `src/pets.ts`. One real screenshot (`docs/screenshots/focus.png`). Names on screen (Mira, Kai) are the fictional seed accounts already in the README screenshots.

**Music:** 120 BPM (beat 0.5s), C major lo-fi chiptune: Cmaj7 → Am7 → Fmaj7 → G. Keys only in the hook; drums drop in at the reveal. Ends on the game's own reward chime (C–E–G). All SFX (coin plings, card blips, swishes) in C major on the beat grid, mixed under the music.
