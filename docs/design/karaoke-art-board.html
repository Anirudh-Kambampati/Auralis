Karaoke, with a voice

Design draft — nothing implemented yet art pixels, 16×16 tiles

Three new karaoke styles, a mic stand that means sing, a singing pose with a mic hand and a bobbing open-mouth face, floating ♪ ♫, a spotlight breath that stays under the calm palette, pets that stop to watch or bounce along, and a quiet upgrade when a microphone is set down beside the singer. Everything below is drawn at 7–10× so the pixels are readable; the strip at the end shows the real in-game size.

1 Karaoke styles

The machine keeps its 1×1 footprint (16 wide, 30 tall, 12 above the tile). The original stays style 0; the three new looks are looks: ['Retro booth', 'Neon pop', 'Cute pastel'], stored as s = 1..3 and drawn as ALT.karaoke[s - 1], the same path as the other styled furniture. Owner colour still recolours each style — here shown in its own default, and in the owner's colour underneath.

2 Mic stand, and what “sing” means

ITEMS.microphone.use and ITEMS.karaoke.use both become 'sing'. USES is derived from item use values, so no list is hand-maintained — but the client's MUSIC_USES set (the “music is shared with the house” tour) must gain 'sing', and the shop tooltip phrase becomes Sing. The stand's silhouette is unchanged; while a character is singing it gains a soft mint badge and a faint ring at the base, nothing louder.

4 In the room

A stage beam comes down from the ceiling into a bright pool on the tile, dust drifts through it, and the singer loops under a halo. Flip the two toggles to see the microphone-adjacent duet and a pet that decided to dance. The follow-up pet behaviour is deterministic: the same clock slot, room and pet id always give the same watch/bounce choice, so every viewer sees it the same way.

5 Pets

Two idle behaviours slot into the existing pet action choice. Watch: turn toward the singer, sit still, ears up, one paw lifted — no pathing change. Dance: a 1 px bounce on the beat with the tail up and paws alternating. Both are borrowed from the pet's current wander seed, so there is no new network field. If the mic is adjacent to the singer, pets prefer watch over dance, and the singer gets the duet pose.

Spotlight src/draw.ts A ceiling fixture with a lit lens, a dithered cone with a brighter core and 1 px edges, halo behind the head, four nested pool ellipses at 0.10–0.26 alpha, six drifting motes, 2.4 s breath. Wider cone and pool on a duet; still no bloom or screen flash.
Duet / stage src/draw.ts (render only) If any microphone sits within 1 tile of the singer: duet pose, a second spotlight, two note streams, a 40×3 px warm stage strip. Nothing is sent to the server or stored.
Pet watch / dance src/pets.ts, src/main.ts When a character sings within 4 tiles: prefer watch (or dance if bond ≥ 2). Chosen from seedOf(pet) + clock slot so it is identical on every client; no state.
Open questions before code
  • Should both items become sing, or the mic stand stay music? Recommendation: both — one action, one tooltip, and USES still derives itself.
  • Is “duet” allowed to pair with a pet instead of a second character, or is it strictly a pose/stage upgrade for one singer? Drawn as the latter.
  • Should the pet preference depend on bond, or just distance? Drawn as distance-first with bond as a tie-break.
  • Style names final? “Neon pop” sits slightly louder than the palette; if it reads too bright we can drop the glow alpha and arrow to 0.06.
