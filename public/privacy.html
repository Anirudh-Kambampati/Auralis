Privacy policy

Auralis · Effective 1 November 2026

Auralis is a study app where you focus on timed sessions, keep to-dos, chat with housemates and decorate a pixel room. This page explains what we store, why, and who else handles it. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.

What we store

  • Your account: username, email address, and your password, which is stored only as a salted scrypt hash. Your email is used to let you log in; we don't send you marketing.
  • Your game and study data: your character, rooms and furniture, coins, pets, focus sessions (when, how long and the subject you typed), exam dates, to-dos, calendar events, and room snapshots (pictures of your room that you choose to save).
  • Messages: house chat, private messages between housemates, notes left on desks, and house to-dos and calendar events. Housemates can see what you share with the house; private messages are visible to the two people in them.
  • Sign-in sessions: a login token so you stay signed in. The server keeps only a hash of it; the token itself stays on your device.
  • On your device: the login token and preferences such as zoom, sound and which to-do list you last viewed, kept in your browser's local storage.
  • Connection details: your IP address is seen by our servers when you connect and is used briefly, in memory, to limit repeated login attempts. It isn't stored.

Who else handles it

  • Neon stores the database and saved room snapshots.
  • Render runs the game server and Vercel serves the app's pages.
  • Google Fonts provides the app's font, so Google sees your IP address when the font loads.
  • Video sites: if someone in your house starts a "watch together" video, the player from that site (YouTube, Vimeo, Twitch or Dailymotion) loads in the app and follows that site's own privacy policy.

These providers process data on our behalf to run the app. All connections to Auralis are encrypted (HTTPS and secure WebSockets).

How long we keep it

We keep your account and data while your account exists. Some lists are trimmed automatically: the oldest chat messages, private messages, desk notes and calendar entries drop off as newer ones are added.
