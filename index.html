what's inside

What is in the house today

No roadmap and nothing "coming soon": every line below is something the app does now, in the room with you.

  • FocusPick a subject and a length. Every finished minute pays a coin.
  • Coins and streaks3, 7 and 30 days in a row add 10%, 25% and 50%. Studying beside a housemate pays you both more, up to double.
  • DecorateDrag, rotate, flip, recolour, put away. Wallpaper, pattern, floor and floor style are yours to change.
  • Rooms that join upThree rooms each, and a bigger one costs coins. Your house code opens everyone's rooms into one house.
  • Chat, DMs and groupsHouse chat, private messages and groups, with pictures, replies and a colour per conversation.
  • To-dosHouse or private, with a date, a time, a repeat and a priority. Finishing one pays the house.
  • Bell and remindersA reminder at the time you set, a pop-up if you allow it, and push while the app is closed.
  • Calendar and announcementsA house calendar and a private one, with exams on them. The owner can pin announcements.
  • Watch partiesYouTube, Vimeo, Twitch or Dailymotion, in step with the house, a group or one friend.
  • PetsFour at most, wandering and napping on their own. A bonded pet does tricks and finds coins.
  • Making thingsFive making pieces turn out a random thing every 20 minutes. The claw drops a prize for 5 coins.
  • Drinks, music and giftsCoffee pays an extra coin a minute. Instruments play and layer. Gift a housemate 10 coins, five times a day.
